About Student Performance Analytics Portal

A modern academic management system designed for Students, Teachers, and Administrators.

Our Mission

The Student Performance Analytics Portal is designed to simplify academic management by providing a centralized platform for monitoring student performance, attendance, assignments, and reports.

The portal enables students, teachers, and administrators to access important academic information quickly through secure authentication and role-based dashboards.

Portal Features

🔐 Secure Authentication

User registration, login, password recovery, session timeout, and secure logout.

📊 Analytics Dashboard

Interactive charts, live statistics, attendance monitoring, and performance prediction.

🔔 Smart Notifications

Real-time notifications with search, filtering, history, import/export, and unread tracking.

👤 Advanced Profile

Auto-save profile, image upload, completion tracker, achievements, and activity history.
